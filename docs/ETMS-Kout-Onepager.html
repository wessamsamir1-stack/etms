01 · The problem we solve

Transportation runs on phone calls — and it costs you every morning.

For a group like Kout — many branches, restaurants, and staff accommodations — the day begins with a fragile, manual operation that decides whether shifts start full and on time. When it slips, the cost is real but hidden, spread across HR, operations, and payroll where no one owns it.

No live visibility

No one knows where the bus is or when it will arrive — employees wait, or miss it entirely.

Last-minute phone calls

Drivers are coordinated by call, from memory — no routes, no record, no accountability.

Manual attendance

Boarding is logged by hand or not at all — creating monthly payroll disputes and lost trust.

Late shifts & idle pay

One late bus short-staffs a line; present staff stand idle, and overtime is authorized to recover.

No routing or utilization

Empty seats and inefficient routes quietly burn fuel — with no report that ever reveals it.

HR firefighting

Managers spend the first hour of the day chasing buses instead of leading their teams.

None of it appears on an invoice.

The most expensive costs in transportation are the ones you never see billed — which is exactly why they are never fixed.
